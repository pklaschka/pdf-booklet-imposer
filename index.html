Sheet & margins
Width × height mm mm

The booklet, including bleed and crop marks, is scaled to stay inside the margins. Use your printer's non-printable border (typically 4–5 mm).

Bleed & crop marks

Marks never start inside the bleed, even if the distance is smaller.

Creep & centre gutter

Inner sheets are shifted towards the spine by one paper thickness each, so the outer margins stay equal after the fore-edge is trimmed. The automatic gutter opens the outer sheets just enough that nothing disappears into the fold. Turn creep off if you will not trim.

Sheet orientation
Output

Booklets need a short-edge flip. If your printer or driver only flips on the long edge, pick that here and every back side is turned upside down to match. Front/back-only output is for manual duplexing.

Back-side offset

X / Y mm mm

Where the back print lands relative to the front, seen from the back (exaggerated). The booklet's back sides are moved the opposite way to cancel it.

Measuring it

  1. Download the calibration sheet and print it double-sided at 100 %, flipping as chosen above.
  2. Hold it against a light, back side facing you, and read where the front cross hits the two rulers.
  3. Add the readings to the X and Y values here. Reprint to check: both lines should sit on 0.
